HEAD CRUSH - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Introduction

HEAD CRUSH ("we", "our", or "the app") is a puzzle game developed by Sakiv. This policy explains what information the app handles and how you can control or delete it.

Playing as a Guest

You can play without an account. In guest mode, game progress and settings stay on your device and nothing is sent to our servers.

Information We Collect When You Create an Account

Creating an account is optional. If you sign in with email/password or with Google, we collect:

  • Email address and, for Google sign-in, your Google display name and profile photo URL.
  • A user ID generated by Firebase Authentication. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see or store them.
  • Game progress (levels completed, stars, high scores, lives, settings) and the username you choose, so your progress can be synced across devices.

This data is stored in Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. It is used only to provide sign-in and progress sync. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties other than Google Firebase acting as our service provider.

Camera and Photo Access

HEAD CRUSH offers an optional "Custom Game" feature that lets you use face photos as in-game pieces. When you use this feature:

  • You pick photos with the Android photo picker or take a photo with your device camera app. The app does not request camera or storage permissions and only receives the photos you choose.
  • Photos are processed on your device using Google ML Kit Face Detection (on-device processing).
  • Face images are stored only in the app's private storage on your device and are never uploaded or shared.
  • Only use photos of people who have agreed to appear in your game. You can delete stored face photos at any time inside the app.

Data Retention and Deletion

  • Delete your account in the app: open your Profile and tap Delete account. This permanently deletes your Firebase account, your email, your cloud-saved progress and your local profile data.
  • Delete without the app: email headcrush.game@gmail.com from the address linked to your account and we will delete your account and cloud data.
  • Guest data and face photos stored on your device are removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore – account sign-in and progress sync (accounts only).
  • Google Sign-In – optional sign-in with your Google account.
  • Google ML Kit Face Detection – runs on your device; images are not sent to Google.

The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

HEAD CRUSH is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.
